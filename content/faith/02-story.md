title: A little of my story

In 2023 I burned out. I stepped away from tech and gave myself time to breathe.

I spent that season with poetry, with painting, with the study of religions, psychology and theology. I went deep into Daniel and Revelation, my favorite books, and the study of prophecy for our times. Along that road I fell in love with God, and became a Seventh-day Adventist.

That time gave me back something I had lost. I came back to my craft with more energy and more love than ever.
