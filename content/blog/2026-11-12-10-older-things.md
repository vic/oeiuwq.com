title: Where I come from
date: 2026-11-12

Before Nix, I spent years in Ruby, Elixir, Scala, Go and Emacs land.

- [params](https://github.com/vic/params) does Ecto-style parameter validation for Elixir. It is still my most-starred repo, go figure.
- [rebecca-theme](https://github.com/vic/rebecca-theme), the purple turtle theme for Spacemacs, the start of my purple everything.
- [gleam-nix](https://github.com/vic/gleam-nix) builds Gleam with Nix.

And I have been lucky to help on other people's projects. I'm an ASF member and was PMC chair of [Apache Buildr](https://buildr.apache.org/), a maintainer and plugin author for [asdf-vm](https://github.com/asdf-vm/asdf), an early [Rubinius](https://github.com/rubinius/rubinius) contributor (I wrote `Enumerable` in Ruby), I wrote the Ruby backends for [Ragel](https://github.com/adrian-thurston/ragel), and lately I send PRs to [jjui](https://github.com/idursun/jjui), an awesome `jj` TUI.

The full list is on [codes](site:codes), on the timeline.
