title: dnx, the project closest to my heart
date: 2026-11-12

[dnx](https://github.com/denful/dnx) is a Nix evaluator I am building from the ground up, as a single Rust binary that needs no root and no system-wide store.

The idea that keeps me up at night: evaluate Nix on interaction nets (Δ-Nets, from [Salvadori's paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.20314)). Reductions on those nets are confluent, so independent work can run in parallel without locks. On top of that I want a cache that keys a computation by what it *means*, not by a file name, so two expressions that mean the same thing share a result.

Where it is today: the core reducer works and is tested, and it evaluates a pure, non-recursive subset of Nix. General recursion is the open problem I am on now, the same hard spot HVM and Bend hit. So this is early research software, and it is the most ambitious thing I have ever tried to build.

It is inspired by projects I admire: Unison, Scala, Effekt, Nix and Lean 4. It needs long, focused, quiet time, and that is the main reason I ask for [sponsors](site:sponsor).
