title: Purple, Rothko, keyboards and a potato
date: 2026-11-12

Not everything here is code, so here is a bit about me.

I love the color [Rebecca purple](https://github.com/vic/rebecca-theme), Rothko paintings, and effect systems, in that order on good days. My Rebecca theme started for Spacemacs and now lives everywhere base16 lives, which is why this whole site is purple by default.

I obsess over my Glove80 keyboard, and over layouts that hurt my fingers less.

In 2023 I burned out and left tech completely. I spent that season with poetry, painting, psychology and theology, deep in Daniel and Revelation. I came back with more love than I left with. I'm a Seventh-day Adventist, and I believe LOVE is what created us. That's why there is a [faith](site:faith) corner and a [love](site:love) corner on this site.

I'm hard of hearing, so open source has been my voice for a long time. Written and async is where I feel at home.

And if you just want to say hi, [leave a potato](https://github.com/vic/vic/issues/new?title=Here%27s%20a%20potato). Just say *"Hey, here's a potato."*
