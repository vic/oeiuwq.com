title: srs, because I was tired of waiting for Rust
date: 2026-11-12

I was tired of Rust compile times and of `target` filling my disk. I have also always loved Scala 3. So [srs](https://github.com/vic/srs) is my take on a Scala 3-like language that compiles to portable C.

Memory is not managed by a garbage collector. srs tracks it with flow effects and stateful protocols, so the compiler knows when a value is alive, borrowed or gone. Effects and handlers come from the same ideas as my [fx-rs](https://github.com/denful/fx-rs) library.

The first goal is for srs to compile itself, and to do it fast. It is very young and unstable, I like to say it is built with `-O fun`: optimized for fun.
