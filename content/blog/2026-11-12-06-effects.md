title: Algebraic effects in Nix, Rust and Go
date: 2026-11-12

Effect systems are one of my favorite ideas in programming. You write code that *asks* for things (read a config, log, fail, call the network) and somebody else *handles* those requests. Your logic stays pure and testable, and the handler decides what really happens.

I have built them in a few languages:

- [nfx](https://github.com/denful/nfx): algebraic effects with handlers, for Nix.
- [fx-rs](https://github.com/denful/fx-rs): ability-style effects for stable Rust, after Scala's Kyo.
- [fx.go](https://github.com/denful/fx.go): effect handlers for Go.
- [zio-logic](https://github.com/vic/zio-logic): μKanren-style logic programming for ZIO, from my Scala days.

If you want to dive in, I keep a fork of the [effects bibliography](https://github.com/vic/effects-bibliography), a big collaborative list of papers on computational effects.
