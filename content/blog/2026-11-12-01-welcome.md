title: Welcome to the BBS, and a map of this site
date: 2026-11-12

Hi, I'm Vic :)

This site is a little bit of every computer I loved growing up. Each corner of it lives on its own subdomain and has its own look, and some of the fun stuff is a click or a key away, so here is the map.

- [oeiuwq.com](site:home) boots like an old PC. Press `DEL` at boot to open SETUP and pick any of 600+ base16 color schemes. Rebecca purple is the default, of course. Your pick follows you to every other corner.
- [codes](site:codes) is a Mac OS 9 desktop with every public repo I have made since 2008 on a timeline. Open the **Logic** window and you can ask questions about my repos, my stars and GH Archive with Datalog or μKanren, right in your browser.
- [projects](site:projects) is a Borland-style DOS IDE where every project of mine is a source file you can open.
- [smalltalk](site:smalltalk) is a little Smalltalk-80. Define classes and methods in the System Browser, and they stay in your browser for next time.
- [nes](site:nes) runs NEON VIGIL, a small 8-bit game: a night courier, a plasma lash, and a code vault to set free.
- [adventure](site:adventure) is a text adventure on a Commodore screen. Type `take lamp`, `throw potato at drone`, and so on.
- [stars](site:stars) is a Gopher client on a GNOME 1 desktop. It searches the README of every repo I made or starred. I use it to find that repo I starred five years ago.
- [portfolio](site:portfolio) has my CV, on NeXTSTEP.
- [faith](site:faith) is a HyperCard stack.
- [love](site:love) is a love letter in DOS EDIT. Write one back with `F2`, it reaches me.
- [sponsor](site:sponsor) is an arcade. Insert a coin and play, you can't lose.

Every game here has an **edit** button. Change the level, the story or the rules, press Run, and play your own version. Your changes stay in your browser.

Use the number keys here to read a message, and `Q` to come back.
