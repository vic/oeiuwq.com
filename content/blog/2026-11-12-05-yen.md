title: Yen, Nix fleets you can ask questions about
date: 2026-11-12

When you run more than a couple of machines with Nix, you want to know things before you deploy. Which hosts open this port ? Who can reach that service ? Does every machine have backups ?

[Yen](https://github.com/vic/yen) is a pure-Nix language for fleet configurations where everything is a query. It has two logic engines inside: μKanren to *synthesize* configuration, and Datalog to *prove* invariants about it. You write the fleet, then you ask it questions and prove things, all before anything touches a server.

Some related bits:

- [ren](https://github.com/vic/ren) is my den framework reimagined as a library on top of [gen](https://github.com/sini/gen).
- [dungeon](https://github.com/vic/dungeon) rebuilds den's API on gen libraries. It is very much in progress and most of its tests are not there yet.

btw, the Logic window on [codes](site:codes#logic) uses the same two kinds of engine, so you can play with Datalog and μKanren over my repos.
