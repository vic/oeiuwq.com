title: Why I ask for sponsors
date: 2026-11-12

I have written open source since 1999, and almost everything I know I learned by reading other people's code. For me, making things for others is an act of love, and that's the real reason I do it.

Right now I give this work my time, my focus, and my own money. I do it gladly. But I want to do it full time, not only on stolen nights, and [dnx](site:blog#2026-11-12-03-dnx) especially needs long, quiet, uninterrupted time to become real.

My goal is **100 monthly sponsors** on [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/vic). Today ten wonderful people are there. Any amount, big or small, gets me closer. Your support goes straight into more hours on dnx and the denful ecosystem, better docs, faster fixes, and keeping all of it free and libre.

There is also my [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/oeiuwq). There I'm saving for a new laptop, and you can commission me there, for software or for spirituality.

Nobody owes me anything. I give freely, and I would love to receive freely too, by your own decision.

If money is not possible right now, that is completely fine. Star the projects you like, share them, open issues, send code, or just say hi. And please sponsor other open source authors too. We are all in this together.

Peace, <3
